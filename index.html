🎯 Compliance Scoring Framework

Scoring Methodology

Consistently rate risk scores, assign gap statuses, and track compliance metrics across your organisation.

1. Risk Score (1–5)

1
Negligible
Minimal enforcement likelihood; no sensitive data; awareness item.
2
Low
Low probability; limited data volume; procedural gap; no immediate impact.
3
Medium
Moderate risk; medium data volume or sensitivity; needs addressing.
4
High
High enforcement probability; large data volume; significant exposure.
5
Critical
Severe risk; highly sensitive data; immediate regulatory exposure.

2. Gap Status Definitions

●
Not Assessed
Not yet reviewed. Starting position — no evidence required.
●
Gap — Critical
Critical risk gap. Immediate escalation. Executive notification required.
●
Gap — High
High risk gap. Urgent action. Owner must be assigned.
●
Gap — Medium
Medium risk. Planned action required. Document assessment.
◐
Partial
Remediation underway. Evidence of progress. Interim controls documented.
✓
Fully Addressed
Requirement met. Evidence filed. No residual gap. Audit sign-off.

3. Compliance Score Formulas

Overall % Addressed
